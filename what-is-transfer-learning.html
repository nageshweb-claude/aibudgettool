What is Transfer Learning in AI?

Published 19 June 2026 · 2 min read

Transfer learning lets a model trained on one task quickly adapt to a related task using far less data.

The Concept

A model trained on millions of images can be quickly adapted for medical imaging with just hundreds of examples. The general knowledge transfers to the specific task.

Why It Matters

Without transfer learning, every AI application would need millions of examples. With it, you can build useful AI with much less data. All LLMs are transfer learning, pre-trained generally, then adapted.

Questions people ask

What is transfer learning?

Transfer learning is using knowledge from a model trained on one task to quickly adapt it to a related task with less data.
