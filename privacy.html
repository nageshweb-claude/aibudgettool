Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Short version: we do not ask for an account, and calculator inputs never leave your device. A few things are stored in your own browser to make the quiz and prompt wheel work, and some outside services (fonts, hosting, the contact form and, in future, ads) receive the technical data any website visit sends.

1. What you type into calculators

Loan amounts, salaries, dates and other inputs are calculated in your browser with JavaScript. They are not sent to our servers. When you change a value, the page address updates so you can bookmark or share the result. Anyone you send that link to can see those values, so don't share a link that contains anything private.

2. What is stored in your browser

The AI hub uses your browser's local storage, which is similar to cookies, for three things: your prompt wheel streak, the quiz levels you have unlocked, and your quiz scores and name for the leaderboard. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clear your browser's site data to delete it.

3. Outside services this site uses

  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, it processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads.
  • Formspree delivers contact form messages. It stores your name, email and message so they can be delivered to us. We use them only to reply.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly, aggregated analytics such as page view counts from our host. These do not identify you personally.

5. Advertising

We plan to show ads from Google AdSense. When ads are live, Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings, and read how Google uses data in its partner sites policy. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

6. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Links to other sites

Guides link to official sources and other websites. Their privacy practices are their own.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page when anything changes and show the new date at the top. Questions about privacy? Use the contact form.
