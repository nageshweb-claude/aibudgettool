What is an Agentic Workflow?

Published 19 June 2026 · 2 min read

An agentic workflow chains multiple AI actions together to complete a complex task end-to-end.

Definition

An agentic workflow is a sequence of AI-driven steps — research, draft, review, execute — chained together so a complex task completes with minimal human intervention.

Example

A customer email arrives. An agent reads it, checks order status via API, drafts a response, and only flags it for human approval if it involves a refund over a threshold.

Why It Matters for Business

Agentic workflows can handle repetitive multi-step processes — lead qualification, expense report review, content publishing pipelines — at a fraction of the manual cost.

Questions people ask
