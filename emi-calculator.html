How your monthly payment is worked out

Most loans in the US, from 30-year mortgages to auto loans, are amortized: you pay the same amount every month, and each payment covers that month's interest first, then reduces what you owe.

M = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)
  1. P is the amount you borrow (the principal).
  2. r is the monthly interest rate: the annual rate divided by 12, then by 100. So 6% a year becomes 0.005.
  3. n is the number of monthly payments. A 30-year mortgage has 360.

In the early years most of each payment goes to interest. Open the amortization schedule above to see the split change year by year.

Example

You borrow $250,000 at 6% a year for 30 years (360 payments).

Monthly rate r = 6 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.005.

Monthly payment = $1,498.88.

Over 360 payments you pay $539,595 in total, so the interest is $289,595.

Three ways to pay less interest

Choose the shortest term you can afford. At 6.5%, a $300,000 mortgage costs about $382,633 in interest over 30 years, but about $170,398 over 15 years.

Make extra principal payments early. Extra money in the first few years cuts the balance while interest is still the biggest part of each payment. Check that your lender has no prepayment penalty.

Compare the APR, not just the payment. A longer term can make an expensive loan look cheap. Look at total interest and fees before you sign.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate a monthly loan payment?

Use M = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly payments. The calculator above does this instantly as you type.

Does the mortgage payment include taxes and insurance?

No. It shows principal and interest only. A typical US mortgage payment also includes property tax, homeowners insurance and sometimes PMI or HOA fees (often called PITI). Add those monthly amounts to the result to estimate your full payment.

Does this work for auto, personal and student loans?

Yes. All standard fixed-rate amortizing loans use the same formula. Use the quick-start buttons for typical mortgage, auto, personal and student loan settings, then change them to match your offer.

Why is so much of my early payment interest?

Interest is charged on the balance you still owe. At the start the balance is highest, so the interest part is biggest. As the balance falls, more of each payment goes to principal.

Is a 15-year or 30-year mortgage better?

A 30-year mortgage has a lower monthly payment, but you pay far more interest in total. A 15-year loan costs more each month and much less overall. Pick the shortest term whose payment fits comfortably in your budget.

Is this the same as an EMI calculator?

Yes. EMI (equated monthly instalment) is the name used in India for a fixed monthly loan payment. Choose INR in the currency menu to see results in rupees with lakh and crore grouping.

Can I use other currencies?

Yes. Pick any of 22 currencies from the menu next to the loan amount. The maths is the same; only the symbol and number grouping change. Your choice is remembered on this device.
