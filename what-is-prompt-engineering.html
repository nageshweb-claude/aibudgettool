Published 19 June 2026 · 2 min read

What Is It?

Prompt engineering is writing effective AI instructions. The same AI gives mediocre or brilliant answers depending on how you ask.

Core Techniques

Role: 'Act as a senior data scientist.' Chain-of-Thought: 'Think step by step.' Few-Shot: Give 2-3 examples. Constraints: What NOT to do. Format: 'Respond as JSON.'
