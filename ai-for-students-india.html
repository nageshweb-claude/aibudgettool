AI Tools for Students in India: Free Guide

Published 19 June 2026 · 2 min read

Indian students can use AI tools completely free to study smarter and prepare for exams. Complete guide inside.

Best Free AI Tools

ChatGPT — General studying, explaining concepts. Gemini — Research, integrates with Google Docs. Claude — Coding assignments, long documents. Perplexity — Research with cited sources. All free.

How to Study with AI

Use the Feynman Technique: Ask AI to explain simply. Explain it back. Have AI find gaps. Re-study only gaps. Generate custom quizzes: 'Give me 20 MCQs on [TOPIC] at [DIFFICULTY].' Summarize textbook chapters in Claude (1M context).

Warning

Never submit AI text as your own work. Most Indian universities use AI detection. Use AI to learn, not to copy.

Budget Tip

All tools above are free. No subscriptions needed. Use our Budget Calculator to track expenses as a student.
