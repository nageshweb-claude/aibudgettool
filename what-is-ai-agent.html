What is an AI Agent? Simple Explanation

Published 19 June 2026 · 2 min read

AI agents go beyond chatbots by taking autonomous actions to complete multi-step tasks independently.

Agent vs Chatbot

Chatbot waits and answers. Agent takes a goal, breaks it into steps, uses tools, and executes autonomously.

2026 Agents

Claude Code, Gemini Spark, OpenAI Operator, Devin. All can work independently on complex tasks.

Risk

Autonomous mistakes compound. Production agents need guardrails and human-in-the-loop checkpoints.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot only answers questions, while an AI agent autonomously plans and executes multi-step tasks using tools.
