What is the Attention Mechanism in AI?

Published 19 June 2026 · 2 min read

Attention lets AI weigh the relevance of every word to every other word, enabling it to understand context correctly.

Simple Explanation

When reading 'The cat sat on the mat because it was tired,' attention helps the model understand that 'it' refers to 'cat' not 'mat.' Each word looks at ALL other words and decides which are most relevant.

Why It Matters

Attention is the core innovation of Transformers (2017). It replaced sequential processing with parallel processing. This is why modern AI is so fast and powerful.
