10 AI Prompt Templates for Business Owners

Published 19 June 2026 · 2 min read

These 10 business prompt templates work with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to save hours on common tasks.

Marketing

'Write 5 social media captions for [PRODUCT] targeting [AUDIENCE], tone: [TONE].' 'Generate 10 blog post ideas for [INDUSTRY] targeting [KEYWORD].'

Customer Service

'Draft a polite response to this complaint: [PASTE]. Acknowledge the issue, apologize once, offer a solution.'

Analysis

'Analyze this sales data and identify the 3 most actionable trends: [DATA].' 'Summarize this contract and flag any unusual clauses: [DOCUMENT].'

Planning

'Create a 30-day content calendar for [BUSINESS TYPE] focused on [GOAL].'

Questions people ask

Yes, ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all offer free tiers powerful enough for most small business marketing and analysis tasks.
