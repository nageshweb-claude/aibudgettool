How the converter handles daylight saving

Countries change their clocks on different dates, and some never change them. A fixed table of offsets goes wrong twice a year, so this converter doesn't use one.

  1. You pick a date and time in the first place.
  2. The converter asks your browser's built-in time zone database (the IANA database, which phones and computers use) what the UTC offset was in that place at that moment.
  3. It then reads the local time in the second place at the same instant, so summer time is applied only where and when it really applies.

Tip: choose a city, not an abbreviation. "CST" can mean US Central time or China Standard Time, and "IST" can mean India, Ireland or Israel.

Example

A team call is at 7:30 PM in India on a date in January.

New York is on standard time (EST) in winter, which is 10 hours 30 minutes behind India.

So the call is at 9:00 AM in New York.

In July, New York is on EDT, only 9 hours 30 minutes behind, so the same 7:30 PM call is at 10:00 AM there.

Questions people ask

What is the time difference between India and the USA?

India (IST) is 10 hours 30 minutes ahead of New York when New York is on standard time (EST), and 9 hours 30 minutes ahead during US daylight saving time (EDT). For Los Angeles it is 13 hours 30 minutes (PST) or 12 hours 30 minutes (PDT).

Does India have daylight saving time?

No. India Standard Time stays at UTC+5:30 all year, so the gap to countries that change their clocks shifts by an hour twice a year.

When do the US and Europe change their clocks?

The US moves clocks forward on the second Sunday of March and back on the first Sunday of November. The UK and EU move forward on the last Sunday of March and back on the last Sunday of October. For a few weeks each year the gap between them is an hour different from usual.

How do I find a good meeting time across time zones?

Look at the timeline under the result. Hours shaded green are 9 AM to 6 PM in each place. Where both rows are green, both people are in working hours.

Why does the converter show a different date?

If the converted time crosses midnight, the date changes too. The result tells you when it is the next day or the day before.

Can I share a converted time?

Yes. The address bar updates as you change the settings. Use Share this time, or send it on WhatsApp, and the other person sees the same conversion.
