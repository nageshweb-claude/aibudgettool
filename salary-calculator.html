How the conversion works

Everything is first turned into a yearly figure, then divided back down. A year is counted as 52 working weeks.

  1. Hourly to yearly: hourly rate × hours a week × 52.
  2. Monthly to yearly: monthly pay × 12.
  3. Yearly to daily: yearly pay ÷ (days a week × 52).
  4. Yearly to hourly: yearly pay ÷ (hours a week × 52).

In India, salaries are often quoted as LPA, lakh per annum. ₹12 LPA means ₹12,00,000 a year before tax, or ₹1,00,000 a month.

Example

You earn ₹60,000 a month and work 40 hours over 5 days a week.

Yearly: ₹60,000 × 12 = ₹7,20,000, which is 7.2 LPA.

Hourly: ₹7,20,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = ₹346 an hour.

Questions people ask

What is 12 LPA per month?

12 LPA is ₹12,00,000 a year, which is ₹1,00,000 a month before deductions. Your in-hand pay is lower once income tax, provident fund and professional tax are taken out, often by 10% to 25% depending on your salary structure and tax regime.

How do I convert my monthly salary to yearly?

Multiply your monthly salary by 12. For example, ₹50,000 a month is ₹6,00,000 a year, which is 6 LPA.

What does LPA mean?

LPA means lakh per annum: your yearly pay in lakhs of rupees. 1 lakh is 1,00,000, so 10 LPA is ₹10,00,000 a year before tax.

How do I work out my hourly rate from a salary?

Divide your yearly salary by the hours you work in a year. For a 40-hour week that is yearly salary ÷ 2,080.

Is this before or after tax?

Before tax. The calculator converts gross pay between time periods. Your take-home pay depends on income tax, provident fund and other deductions where you live.

What if I work part-time or six days a week?

Move the hours and days sliders to match your schedule. The hourly and daily figures update instantly.

Does the currency change the result?

No. The currency menu only changes the symbol and digit grouping. No exchange rate is applied.
