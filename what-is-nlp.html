What is NLP? Natural Language Processing

Published 19 June 2026 · 2 min read

NLP is the AI field that enables computers to understand and generate human language.

What is NLP?

AI field enabling computers to understand and generate human language. Every time you use Siri, Google Translate, or ChatGPT, NLP is working.

Key Tasks

Text classification (spam), sentiment analysis, translation, summarization, question answering, text generation. Modern LLMs handle all with one model.

Questions people ask

What does NLP stand for?

Natural Language Processing, the AI field focused on understanding and generating human language.
