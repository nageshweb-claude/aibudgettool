About AI Budget Tool

A free learning and productivity hub built around two simple tools: a daily AI Prompt Wheel and a practical AI Budget Calculator.

Our Mission

AI Budget Tool helps students, creators, freelancers and small business owners learn AI by doing. The Prompt Wheel gives one useful prompt at a time, while the budget calculator makes personal finance quick, private and easy.

The site is free, no-login and browser-based. You can spin prompts, test AI knowledge, read beginner guides and calculate your savings rate without creating an account.

What We Offer
AI Prompt Wheel
Daily expert prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity.
AI Budget Calculator
Track income, expenses and savings rate across common global currencies.
AI Quiz
Practice AI fundamentals with questions across multiple categories.
SEO Learning Pages
Short guides on AI terms, tools, safety, prompting, careers and use cases.
Privacy First

The core tools run in your browser. Budget entries are not sent to a server, and the site is designed to work without signup or unnecessary friction.
