About AI Budget Tool

Free calculators and AI guides, built in India.

AI Budget Tool is a free site with everyday calculators and plain-English guides to AI. Everything runs in your browser, with no sign-up.

Why this site exists

Most people search for the same small answers again and again: a loan EMI, what a salary works out to per month, what time it is for a colleague abroad. Many sites that answer them are slow, cluttered or ask for an account. This site answers them instantly and lets you share the result with one link.

The AI side exists for the same reason. Students and working professionals are told to "learn AI" but rarely given a simple starting point. The guides, daily prompt wheel and quiz are that starting point.

What you can use here

  • Loan calculator: what will my monthly mortgage or car payment be?
  • Salary converter: what is $60,000 a year per hour, or $25 an hour a year?
  • Time zone converter: what time is it in LA when it's 3 PM in New York?
  • Age calculator: how old am I exactly, and when is my next birthday?
  • Download time calculator: how long will this file take to download or upload?
  • Unit converter: how many inches, pounds, MB or mph is that?
  • AI hub: a daily prompt wheel, a 250-question AI quiz, a 5-week beginner course, prompt engineering lessons, an AI model comparison and a monthly budget planner
  • AI guides: short explainers on how AI works and which tools to use

How we keep things accurate

Calculator formulas are standard and shown on each page, with a worked example you can check. AI tools change quickly, so comparison pages show the date they were last checked and link to the official sources. If you spot a mistake, please tell us and we will fix it.

Who runs it

AI Budget Tool is built and run by an independent developer based in India.

Privacy in one line

The numbers you type into a calculator stay on your device. The privacy policy explains exactly what is and isn't stored.
