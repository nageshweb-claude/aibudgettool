How your age is counted

Age is counted the way people say it: full years first, then full months, then the days left over.

  1. Count the whole years from your birthday to the chosen date.
  2. Count the whole months after your last birthday.
  3. Count the remaining days, using the real length of each month, including 28 or 29 days for February.

Born on 29 February? In years without a 29th, this calculator counts your birthday on 28 February.

Example

Born on August 15, 1998. What is the age on January 1, 2027?

Aug 15, 1998 to Aug 15, 2026 is 28 years. Aug 15 to Dec 15 is 4 months. Dec 15 to Jan 1 is 17 days.

Age: 28 years, 4 months and 17 days.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate my exact age?

Enter your date of birth. The calculator counts full years, then full months, then remaining days up to today, or up to any date you choose.

How do I find my age on a specific date for an exam form?

Many exam and job forms ask for your age as on a cut-off date. Set the second date to that cut-off and the calculator shows your age on that day.

How many days old am I?

The total days figure under your result shows exactly how many days have passed since you were born, counting leap years.

What day of the week was I born?

It appears under the result as "You were born on a".

What if I was born on 29 February?

In years without 29 February, the calculator treats 28 February as your birthday when counting down to your next one.

Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Your date only appears in the page address so you can share or bookmark the result.
