What is AI Hallucination? Why AI Makes Things Up

Published 19 June 2026 · 2 min read

AI hallucination is when AI confidently generates false information. Here is why it happens and how to protect yourself.

What is Hallucination?

When AI confidently says something false — fake studies, invented quotes, non-existent citations. It is not lying — it is generating statistically likely text that happens to be wrong.

Why It Happens

LLMs predict likely next words from patterns. They cannot distinguish true patterns from false ones. Plausible-sounding = generated, regardless of truth.

Protection

Always verify important claims. Use Perplexity for sourced research. Ask 'are you sure?' RAG reduces hallucination by grounding in real data.

Questions people ask

Why does AI hallucinate?

AI predicts statistically likely text rather than verified truth, so it can generate plausible but false information confidently.
