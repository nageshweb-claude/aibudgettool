How the converter works

Each value is first converted to one base unit (metres, kilograms, bytes or metres per second), then from the base unit to the unit you want. That keeps every pair exact and consistent.

  1. Your value × the "from" unit's size in the base unit.
  2. That result ÷ the "to" unit's size in the base unit.
  3. Temperature uses formulas instead, because 0 °C is not 0 °F.

Data has two families of units. KB, MB and GB step up by 1000; KiB, MiB and GiB step up by 1024. That is why a "256 GB" phone shows about 238 GiB of space.

Questions people ask

How many centimetres are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54 centimetres. To convert inches to cm, multiply by 2.54. To convert cm to inches, divide by 2.54.

How do I convert kg to pounds?

Multiply kilograms by 2.20462. For example, 70 kg is about 154.3 lb.

How many MB are in a GB?

1,000 MB in decimal units, which disk makers and internet providers use. In binary units, 1 GiB is 1,024 MiB.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5, then add 32. So 25 °C is 77 °F, and 37 °C is 98.6 °F.

What is a knot?

A knot is one nautical mile per hour, about 1.852 km/h or 1.151 mph. It is used for ships and aircraft.
