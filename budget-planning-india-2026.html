Budget Planning Guide India 2026

Published 19 June 2026 · 2 min read

The 50/30/20 budgeting rule, adapted for Indian cities, helps you plan needs, wants, and savings effectively.

50/30/20 for India

50% Needs: Rent, food, transport, EMIs. 30% Wants: Entertainment, dining, shopping. 20% Savings: Emergency fund, SIPs, retirement. Adjust for metro cities (60% needs).

Tips

Start SIP at Rs 500/month. Build 3-month emergency fund first. Cooking at home 5 days/week saves Rs 5,000-10,000/month in metros.

Questions people ask

What is the 50/30/20 budgeting rule?

It allocates 50% of income to needs, 30% to wants, and 20% to savings, adjusted upward for needs in expensive Indian metro cities.
